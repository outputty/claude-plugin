# The audit report

Step 11 replies under the style's headings:

**Assumptions**: none of the auditor's own. Each premise is a rule that was read or a fact that was listed, named with the tool that gave it.

**Solution**:

- the findings by rule group, most widespread first, each with its `file:line` and the rule's words;
- the verdict on every rule of step 5: findings, none found, or not checked and why;
- the refactor plan.

**Next**: the open questions, and each refactor that rests on one.
