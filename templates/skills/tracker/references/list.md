# The ticket list

The list prints one line per ticket and a blank line, in this shape.

```text
1. #<n> <title>                     buildable · priority:high

2. #<n> <title>                     blocked by #<m>

3. #<n> <title>                     In Progress · 2 open PRs, 1 merged

4. #<n> <title>                     needs-planning
```

A ticket whose body shows a call or an output gets its before/after in its own fence under its line.
