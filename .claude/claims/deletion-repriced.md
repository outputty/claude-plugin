# Claim: An unpriced deletion verdict can invert on measurement

**Status:** valid · **Validated:** 2026-08-06 · **Scope:** laygo (consumer project) unless stated otherwise

## Statement

A component scoped for deletion was re-priced at ~156 lines confined to the two benefiting packages, buying ~50% on its path — the kill verdict inverted and it stayed. The measurement existed before the kill was written.

## How it was validated

Line counts + benchmark figures from the laygo cycle that scoped the deletion.

## How to revalidate

Price any future deletion before scoping; stale only if the pricing rule itself changes.
